Supervision

What these do
  • Start clears a pause, a kill-switch stop or a quarantine, and starts the supervisor (through launchd when it's installed).
  • Pause / sleep skips engine cycles and holds nothing awake, so the Mac can sleep. Paid orders and free-sample signups keep being handled.
  • Restart reloads the settings you saved.
  • Kill switch stops everything, including the webhook listener, until you press Start.
